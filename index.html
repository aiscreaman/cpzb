03 // Products
Core Products

Main timber products for
commercial use.

Three product categories are presented in this company profile.

Product 01
Solid Pallet

Sturdy solid wood pallets built for stacking, loading, and repeated use in warehouses and logistics operations. Standard and custom sizes available.

Product 02
Laminated Wood

Laminated timber with a smooth, uniform surface — ready for furniture makers, contractors, and interior fit-outs that need consistent sizing and clean edges.

Product 03
Finger Joint

Finger joint timber — shorter pieces joined for strength and minimal waste. Popular with frame fabricators and manufacturers who need structurally sound material without paying for full-length planks.

04 // Production Process
Production Flow

Timber processing stages.
From intake to delivery.

01
Log Selection
Material intake begins with sorting and selecting timber according to usable output requirements.
02
Cutting & Sizing
Logs are cut and dimensioned based on the product format needed for the next stage.
03
Processing
Timber is shaped, arranged, and prepared through the required processing workflow.
04
Treatment
Where required, treatment steps support product readiness, handling standards, and export alignment.
05
Assembly
Components are assembled into finished product form with attention to fit, structure, and repeatability.
06
Quality Check
Output is checked before dispatch so the workflow closes with accountability, not assumptions.
07
Delivery
Once your order is done, we coordinate loading and scheduling so it reaches you when you need it — not when it's convenient for us.
